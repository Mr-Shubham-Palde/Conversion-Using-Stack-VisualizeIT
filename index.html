Operator Precedence Guide

Higher precedence = processed first. Parentheses override everything.

^   Exponentiation Highest (3)
* /   Multiply, Divide Medium (2)
+ -   Add, Subtract Lowest (1)
Postfix: Pop operators with equal or higher priority (left-to-right).
Prefix: Pop only strictly higher priority (handles right-to-left for ^).
